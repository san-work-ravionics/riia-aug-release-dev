RITA (Reinforcement Intelligence Trading Assistant) is a production algorithmic trading system. It runs a Double DQN RL model trained on Nifty 50 equities to generate buy/sell/hold signals, manages F&O portfolio positions with hedging, and provides a full-featured dashboard for monitoring, backtesting, and configuration.
